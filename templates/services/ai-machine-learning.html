FAQ

Common questions about AI development.

Do you build custom AI models or use existing ones?

Both. Most projects are faster and more reliable built on proven foundation models (LLMs, vision models) that we fine-tune, prompt-engineer, and wrap in retrieval or tool-use pipelines. We train custom models only when the problem genuinely needs one.

What is a RAG system and do I need one?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation connects an LLM to your own documents or data so answers are grounded in facts you control, rather than the model's training data. If your product needs to answer questions about your own content accurately, you likely need one.

How long does an AI agent build usually take?

A focused single-purpose agent typically takes 3 to 6 weeks from scoping to production. Multi-agent systems or ones requiring custom tool integrations run longer, depending on the number of systems involved.
