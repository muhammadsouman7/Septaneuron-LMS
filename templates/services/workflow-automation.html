The unglamorous work that quietly costs the most.

Most operational drag isn't one big broken system, it's ten small manual steps between systems that already work fine on their own. We find those seams and close them: data that should move on its own, approvals that shouldn't need a Slack message, reports that shouldn't be built by hand every Monday.

The result is usually a mix of integrations, a lightweight internal tool, and a dashboard, sized to the actual workflow rather than a generic template.

FAQ

Common questions about automation.

What kinds of workflows can you automate?

Anything with a repeatable, rule-based pattern: data entry between systems, approval chains, lead routing, invoice processing, reporting, and notifications across the tools your team already uses.

Do you build on existing automation platforms or from scratch?

Both, depending on the case. Simple integrations often run faster on existing automation tools; anything needing custom logic, higher reliability, or tighter control is built as custom services with proper monitoring.

Can you build an internal dashboard on top of our existing data?

Yes. We regularly build admin panels and business dashboards that pull from your existing databases or third-party APIs, so your team gets visibility without exporting spreadsheets by hand.
