01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Septaneuron collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information connected with this website, our project inquiry process, and our internship portal. Septaneuron is a software and digital product studio based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, providing AI, web development, automation, backend, video editing, SEO, and social media marketing services.

This policy does not replace any privacy notice or data-processing agreement that may apply to a specific client project. A project agreement may contain additional requirements for confidential or regulated information.

02

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you submit our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, company or organization, selected service, budget range, timeline, and project details. We also receive information you choose to send by email.

Technical information. Our hosting provider and security tools may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and error logs. This helps maintain availability, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical issues.

Cookies. The website uses only essential technologies required for basic operation, including a secure session cookie used to keep portal users signed in. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated and any required consent mechanism will be provided.

03

Intern portal

Account data. If you take part in our internship program, we create a portal account using the email address you provide. We store your name, email address, assigned internship domain(s), account role, and sign-in activity such as last login time and failed sign-in attempts. Your password is stored only in hashed form, never in plain text.

Submissions. When you submit work, we store what you provide, such as notes, uploaded files, and links (for example a GitHub repository or a LinkedIn post), together with submission time, status, feedback, and scores given by your domain admin. Content you publish on third-party platforms such as LinkedIn or GitHub is governed by those platforms' own policies.

Emails. We send transactional emails related to your account, such as a welcome message with login details and notifications when a task is posted or updated in your domain. These are part of the program and are not marketing messages.

Access. Your submissions and progress are visible to you and to the administrators responsible for your internship domain and to Septaneuron's super administrators. Portal sessions end automatically after a period of inactivity.

04

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, understand project requirements, prepare proposals, communicate about potential work, provide requested services, and manage our professional relationship.

For the intern portal, we use information to create and secure accounts, assign and deliver tasks, review submissions, give feedback, send program notifications, and evaluate internship participation.

We may also use technical information to secure the website and portal, prevent spam and abuse, troubleshoot errors, improve performance, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use project inquiry or portal details for unrelated advertising.

05

Sharing and service providers

We share information only with trusted providers that help us operate the website, portal, and communications, such as our web hosting provider (Vercel), our database and file storage provider (Supabase), our email service provider, security services, and project collaboration tools. These providers receive only the information needed for their service.

Contact form submissions are sent from our website to our own lead-handling service and delivered to our designated inbox.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of Septaneuron or others, or as part of a business transfer. We do not share your inquiry or portal data with unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, evaluate or manage a potential engagement, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Intern account data and submissions are kept for the duration of the program and for a reasonable period afterwards for records, certificates, and references, unless you ask us to delete them and no legitimate reason requires us to keep them.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, sign-in attempt limits, and restricted access to portal data. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, highly sensitive personal information, or confidential credentials through the public contact form.

07

Your choices and rights

You may choose not to provide optional information, although this may limit our ability to evaluate an inquiry. You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or explain our handling of personal information we hold about you, including your intern account, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification.

You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time. Program emails that are necessary to run your internship may continue while your account is active. To make a privacy request, contact us using the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection law.
