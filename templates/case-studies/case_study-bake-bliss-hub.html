02 / The problem

The challenge was turning limited campaign reporting into actionable decisions without over-claiming business outcomes.

The available dashboard emphasizes delivery and engagement metrics rather than purchases or revenue. That makes it possible to discuss reach, views, conversations, and spend, but not to claim a verified sales or ROI figure from the supplied evidence.

03 / The solution

Use the campaign data to decide where the next rupee should work harder.

The approach is to separate campaign objectives and read each metric in context: retargeting for warm-audience response, prospecting for new-audience reach, and creative optimization where engagement is weaker.

01

Retarget

Prioritize warm audiences when the objective is to drive direct messaging conversations.

02

Prospect

Use prospecting to extend reach while monitoring the cost of generating meaningful engagement.

03

Optimize

Refresh creative and targeting based on the observed conversation and reach patterns.

08 / Outcomes and reflections

A grounded view of campaign performance, without overstating business impact.

The dashboard supports a useful operational conclusion: retargeting is producing more reported messaging conversations and higher reach in the displayed period, while prospecting is operating at lower daily spend.

The next step is to connect campaign data with actual inquiries, orders, revenue, and customer acquisition cost so budget decisions can be tied to business outcomes rather than platform delivery metrics alone.
