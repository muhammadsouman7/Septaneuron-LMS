01 / Introduction

Turning paid social activity into measurable campaign insight.

The report provides a broader campaign comparison than a simple reach dashboard. It makes it possible to examine how retargeting, prospecting, upsell, and awareness differ in clicks, reach, CTR, cost per link click, and spend.

The report does not show which campaigns ultimately produced orders or qualified customers, so the case study treats ad-platform metrics as directional evidence rather than final business outcomes.

02 / The problem

Four campaigns can perform very differently when measured against the same business objective.

The supplied report shows a wide spread in engagement efficiency: retargeting has the highest displayed CTR and lowest cost per link click, while awareness has the lowest CTR and highest cost per link click. Those are platform performance observations, not proof of revenue impact.

The report does not show which campaigns ultimately produced orders or qualified customers, so the case study treats ad-platform metrics as directional evidence rather than final business outcomes.

03 / The solution

Use the campaign data to decide where the next rupee should work harder.

The solution is to read each campaign according to its role, then use comparable metrics to identify where creative, audience strategy, or budget allocation deserves further investigation.

01

Retarget

Use warm-audience campaigns when the objective is to capture users already familiar with the business.

02

Prospect

Use broader audience acquisition to build reach and generate new traffic.

03

Upsell

Evaluate upsell activity separately so additional-value campaigns are not judged only by awareness metrics.

06 / Results

A four-campaign view of Al-Wahad Traders' reported Meta Ads performance.

The supplied report totals 58,877 link clicks, 9,890,289 impressions, 3,922,791 reach, 0.85% CTR, PKR 3.09 cost per link click, and PKR 174,129 amount spent. At campaign level, retargeting reports 29,287 clicks and 0.98% CTR; prospecting reports 21,598 clicks and 2,002,915 reach.

07 / Limitations and scope

Honest about what ad-platform metrics can and can't show.

ATTRIBUTION

No sales data in the supplied report

The image reports ad-platform metrics but does not show revenue, orders, qualified leads, or verified ROAS.

OBJECTIVES

Metrics need campaign context

Awareness, prospecting, upsell, and retargeting have different purposes, so a single metric should not be treated as a universal success measure.

NEXT TEST

Connect clicks to outcomes

The next measurement layer should connect campaign traffic to inquiries, conversions, revenue, and acquisition cost.

08 / Outcomes and reflections

A complete campaign-level performance snapshot with clear next measurement steps.

The report gives a strong operational picture of where clicks, reach, and cost are concentrated. Retargeting has the highest displayed CTR and lowest cost per link click, while prospecting has the highest displayed reach.

To move from platform performance to business performance, the next iteration should connect these campaign records to downstream sales or qualified inquiry data and then evaluate budget changes against verified ROI.
